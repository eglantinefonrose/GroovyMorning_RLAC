# Data Creation for Training AI Models

Artificial intelligence closely depends on data quality. To allow GroovyMorning to detect a radio chronicle precisely, learning the sound and textual characteristics of chronicles was necessary. The construction of a "Ground Truth" from scratch was undertaken.

## The "Ground Truth" Concept

In AI, **Ground Truth** refers to the reference dataset for which the exact answer is known. In this context, this corresponds to thousands of radio segments accompanied by precise labels, for example: "Start of the Geopolitics Note at 08:16:04."

## The Automated Creation Pipeline

Since manual recovery of this data was inconceivable, automation of the process was put in place by exploiting official archives:

1.  **Scraping (Radio France API)**: Official podcasts, already segmented by Radio France, are recovered to serve as reference models.
2.  **Temporal Alignment**: The full radio stream (the non-cut "live") is downloaded in parallel. 
3.  **Audio Cross-Correlation**: Through mathematical signal analysis, the podcast segment is located within the live stream to obtain the exact timecode in live conditions (including surrounding advertisements and jingles).

## From Audio to Text: Dataset Generation

After segment isolation, different types of data are generated for model training:
*   **Audio Data**: Extraction of features (MFCC, Spectrograms) for jingle detection.
*   **Textual Data**: Use of **Whisper** and **kyutai** to obtain synchronized transcriptions (.srt files).
*   **Semantic Labeling**: Identification of transition phrases ("It's 8:15," "See you tomorrow Pierre") with **kyutai** for training NLP models (CamemBERT) by transcribing the first 10 seconds of each show and then extracting the first sentence of this transcription.

## Human-in-the-loop: The Verification Interface

In a previous method, entire radio shows were transcribed and then an LLM tried to locate the different chronicles within the show.
In order to be able to manually verify the LLM detections more quickly, a dedicated interface, **SegmentTimecodeVerification**, was developed to guarantee optimal quality of the test dataset.

This tool allows for human intervention to:
1.  Listen to the segment detected by the AI.
2.  Visualize the transcription in parallel.
3.  Adjust the start/end slider to the millisecond.
4.  Validate or reject the detection.

## Diversity Challenges

Dataset variety was a priority, including:
*   Different voices (diversity of genders, accents).
*   Different times of day (rhythmic morning shows vs. calm night broadcasts).
*   Complex cases (chronicles starting with music, phone interviews).

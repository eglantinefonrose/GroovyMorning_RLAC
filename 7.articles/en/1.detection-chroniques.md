# AI Radio Chronicle Detection: From Raw Audio to Intelligent Segmentation

In the **GroovyMorning** project, one of the major challenges is the automatic identification of the beginning and end of radio chronicles (e.g., "France Inter's 7/10") within an uninterrupted audio stream. This task, located in the `4.rd/1.IAChronicleDetector` module, explores several technological approaches to transform an audio signal into structured metadata.

## A Multi-modal Approach

Rather than relying on a single method, a research and development (R&D) framework was developed to compare several paradigms:

1.  **LLM Approaches (Large Language Models)**: Use of models like **DeepSeek** or **Claude**. The audio is first transcribed, and then the text is sent to the AI to identify semantic cues for start and end points. This is the most semantically accurate method, although it introduces processing latency.
2.  **Classic Machine Learning**: Use of **Random Forest** or **Hybrid** models based on transcription vectors. These models are trained to recognize the textual signature of a radio transition.
3.  **Native Audio Analysis**: Detection based on pre-trained models that directly analyze the audio spectrum to identify jingles or speaker changes.
4.  **Transformers**: Exploration of attention models to capture long-term temporal dependencies in broadcasts.

## The Interoperability Standard

To allow these methods to coexist, a standardized JSON output format was imposed. Each detector must provide:
- A label (chronicle name).
- Start and end timestamps.
- The precise moment the detection was confirmed (`detected_at`).
- A confidence index.

## Live Simulation

One of the most innovative aspects is the **stream simulator**. Instead of processing a static audio file, the audio is cut into buffers of a few seconds to simulate real-time reception. This allows for measuring not only the precision of the AI but also its **responsiveness** (latency), a crucial factor for an "instant" radio-on-demand service.

This architecture allows GroovyMorning to remain agnostic towards AI models: the integration of a new cutting-edge model is facilitated by adhering to this standard interface.

## Selected Solution: Live Transcription and DeepSeek Paid API

### Usage

The DeepSeek API (deepseek-v4-flash) is requested to detect phrases corresponding to the start of chronicles, based on a list of chronicles to be detected (provided in order). The text is analyzed as it arrives, simulating a live stream.
Few-shot prompting is used to provide examples directly in the prompt (examples of chronicle start phrases).

### Gross Error Management

To avoid gross errors, the chronicles are compared with their theoretical schedule. A detected chronicle whose schedule has already passed is ignored.
